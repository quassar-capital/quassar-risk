# Quassar Risk Research

Financial Risk Analytics | Portfolio Exposures | Stress Testing | Scenario Analysis

## Overview

Quassar Risk Research is a research and engineering initiative within Quassar Capital focused on methods for examining financial risk across portfolios and market environments.

The repository is intended to support the development and evaluation of analytical approaches to risk measurement, exposure analysis, stress testing, and scenario-based assessment.

Its scope is methodological rather than advisory. Research materials will emphasize transparent assumptions, reproducible analysis, and the careful interpretation of model outputs.

## Research Scope

### Portfolio Risk

Methods for examining portfolio sensitivities, concentration, volatility, correlation, and potential sources of loss under varying market conditions.

### Exposure Analysis

Approaches to measuring factor, asset-class, and market exposures, including their behavior across different investment environments.

### Stress Testing

Frameworks for evaluating hypothetical adverse conditions, historical market disruptions, and the sensitivity of portfolio outcomes to specified shocks.

### Scenario Analysis

Techniques for constructing and assessing economic and financial scenarios, including changes in interest rates, equity markets, credit conditions, and cross-asset relationships.

### Model Validation

Research practices for examining assumptions, data quality, estimation uncertainty, and the limitations of quantitative risk measures.

## Research Principles

Work developed within this repository will be guided by four principles:

- **Methodological clarity:** Clearly defined assumptions, inputs, and analytical procedures.
- **Reproducibility:** Documented processes that allow research findings to be independently examined.
- **Risk awareness:** Recognition of estimation uncertainty, model limitations, and changing market relationships.
- **Responsible disclosure:** Separation of public research materials from proprietary investment systems and confidential information.

## Development Status

**Phase 0 — Research Planning**

This repository is in its initial planning stage. Research documentation, analytical examples, and technical resources may be introduced following internal review.

No production-ready risk management system, investment model, or trading application is represented by this repository at this stage.

## Scope of Public Materials

Any materials subsequently approved for public release will be limited to research, educational examples, or nonproprietary engineering resources.

The repository is not intended to disclose Quassar Capital's internal portfolio positions, investment signals, proprietary models, trading parameters, confidential datasets, or production infrastructure.
